**Version** [OWNER: version, e.g. 2026-11] · **Applies from** [OWNER: date]

Bookeyond Kid is a learning app for children, used through a parent's or caregiver's account, and by schools that a
family allows. This notice explains what we collect, why, who can see it, how long we keep it and what you can do
about it. We have tried to write it plainly; if anything is unclear, contact us (section 10).

### 2. What we collect

**About you (a parent, caregiver, teacher, principal or administrator):**

- Your email address (needed to sign in and to receive account emails) and your password, which we store only in a
  scrambled form (a hash) that cannot be turned back into the password.
- The language you use the app in.
- A phone number, only if you choose to add and confirm one.
- Administrators only: the key for their authenticator app, stored encrypted.

**About your child - and only what you enter:**

- The name your child is called in the app (it does not have to be their full name) and their date of birth, which we
  use to suggest learning suited to their age.
- The language of your child's own space and the Learning Buddy your child chooses.
- Children do **not** have their own account, email address or phone number, and they cannot upload anything.

**While your child learns:**

- Each answer: the question, what your child answered, whether it was right, how long it took and whether they opened
  the hint. Answers given after opening the hint count as practice, not as proof of a skill.
- What we work out from the answers: how each skill is going, what to practise again (Smart Review), what to do next,
  and the notes we show you about your child's learning.
- How much learning time has been used each day, if you set a daily limit.

**When you use the app:**

- For each signed-in device: the internet (IP) address and the browser's description (its "user agent"), kept with
  the sign-in so you and we can recognise it. Requests to confirm an email, reset a password or change an email keep
  the IP address they came from.
- If you link a child's own device: a description of that device's browser.

**Other things we keep:** the email addresses you invite as caregivers; family and school names; for payments, the
plan, amount, a payment reference and a note - **we never collect card or bank details**; pictures and recordings that
teachers and educators upload for lessons; and a record of important actions (who did what, by account number, and
when), which does not name anyone.

**On our public pages** (the home page and "Try a lesson"), we do not ask for any personal information and nothing a
visitor does is stored.

### 3. Why we use it

- To run your account and your child's learning: sign-in, choosing the next activity from your child's own answers,
  showing you what the evidence says.
- To keep accounts safe: recognising devices, stopping stolen sign-ins, confirming important actions.
- To send you the emails the account needs (confirming your email, resetting a password, caregiver invitations).
- To let a school see a child's learning, **only when the family has allowed that school** and the child is in the
  teacher's class.
- To record payments for plans.

We do **not** use your family's information for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not build profiles for
marketing. [REVIEW 1: the legal basis to name for each use, if the applicable law requires one.]

### 4. Cookies and storage on your device

Bookeyond Kid sets **no cookies**, and uses **no analytics, advertising or tracking tools** and no third-party scripts;
the app's fonts and code come from Bookeyond Kid itself. The app keeps a few things in your browser's storage so that
it works: your sign-in (so you stay signed in), your language, whether sound is on, a linked child device, a
principal's chosen school, and - only until the browser tab is closed - your child's current sitting for break
reminders. They stay on your device. Signing out removes your sign-in; the rest stays until you clear the browser's
data. [REVIEW: whether this storage needs consent or only notice.]

### 7. Your choices and rights

From your account you can, at any time:

- **Download** all of your family's data (your account, your children, their answers including hint use, your plan).
- **Correct** your child's name or date of birth, and your own details.
- **Delete** a child, or your whole account.
- **Allow or withdraw** a school's access to your child.
- Set a **daily time limit** and **break reminders**.
