### 1. What Bookeyond Kid is

A learning app for children. Children learn in their own space; parents and caregivers see what their answers show
and decide how the app is used; schools that a family allows can follow a child's learning in class. Bookeyond Kid
is in **early access**: subjects and features are still being added, and the public page says which subjects are
available now and which are in preparation.

### 2. Accounts

- Accounts are for adults: parents and caregivers, and the teachers, principals and administrators of schools that use
  Bookeyond Kid. [REVIEW 2, 3: minimum age for an account holder and whether it must be checked.]
- Children do not have accounts. A parent or caregiver creates a profile for each child and is responsible for how
  their children use the app.
- Keep your password to yourself. You are responsible for what is done with your account; tell us at once if you
  think someone else has used it. You can sign out every device from your account.
- Give true information about yourself. A child's profile needs only the name the child is called in the app and
  their date of birth.

### 3. Families

- Early access for families has **nothing to pay**; no payment details are asked for. If paid plans are introduced,
  they will be explained before anything is charged and you will choose whether to take one; nothing changes for your
  family without that choice. [OWNER: confirm this commitment before publishing.]
- You decide whether a school may see your child's learning, and you can withdraw that at any time.
- You can download your family's data, and delete a child or your account, at any time (see the privacy notice).

### 4. Schools

- A school's plan is **arranged with the Bookeyond team**; nothing is bought online. Its price, period and what it
  includes are agreed in writing with the school, and payment is made as described on the school's invoice.

### 6. What progress means

The app describes a child's skills from their own answers, says how strong that evidence is, and does not call a
skill secure until it has been shown on more than one day. These descriptions help a family; they are **not an
assessment, diagnosis or certificate**, and Bookeyond Kid does not promise any particular learning result.

We may suspend an account that does any of these, and tell you why unless the law prevents it.

We work to keep Bookeyond Kid available and your child's learning safe, but early-access software can have faults and
interruptions. We may change or stop features; we will not remove your ability to download or delete your data. [OWNER: confirm
