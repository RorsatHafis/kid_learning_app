# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Children aged 3–12** (Cambodian families first; the youngest are pre-readers), often on a parent's phone or a shared tablet, learning in short sessions at home. They can't be expected to read long instructions.
- **Parents / guardians**, who set up the family, add children, and want to understand what their child actually understands and what to do next. Often on a phone, between other things.
- **Teachers** invited by a school, working with classes. **Principals**, who set up a school, its classes and its staff.
- **Administrators / educators**, who author, check and publish content in the Content Studio and operate the platform (staff, accounts, audit log).

## Product Purpose

Bookeyond Kid turns short learning activities into real evidence of what a child understands. The platform decides what each child needs next from their own answers, and explains it to the adult. Success: a child who wants to come back, and a parent who can say, truthfully, what their child can do and why the next step was chosen.

## Positioning

"Content tells what can be learned. Teacher tells how. Platform decides what this child needs next." Same content + different evidence = a different path, with an explanation the adult can read. Completion is never presented as mastery; every parent-facing claim is grounded in counted answers.

## Operating Context

- Child space: journey map, activity intro, questions (multiple choice, true/false, number, short answer, matching), feedback after each answer, completion with the next step, a press-and-hold grown-up gate, sound that can be muted, keyboard play.
- Parent space: family home, child overview (learning now, why this next, skills & evidence with standings Not started / Too early to tell / Needs practice / Progressing / Secure, recent activity with decisions, insights), account (password, sign out everywhere, email confirmation, data export).
- Teacher: invitations, classes. Principal: school setup, classes, staff invitations. Admin: Content Studio (subjects, skills, objectives, questions, activities, curricula, checks, preview, publish, versions), Staff, Accounts (suspend/reinstate), Activity log.
- Real emails (confirmation, reset, invitations). Web app on phones and desktops.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Backend is authoritative for learning, evidence, mastery, progression and security; the frontend never recreates those rules.
- Content today: one published path, "Maths - Junior" (addition, ages 4–9). Khmer and English subjects are **in preparation** — native-authored copy and content are pending (PD-14/15); the UI is English.
- Not built / undecided: plans and pricing (PD-09), children joining school classes (PD-01), content review (PD-20), account deletion (PD-24), legal pages (PD-18), Learning Buddy's name (PD-13).
- No leaderboards, child comparison, virtual currency, loss-aversion streaks, ads, social features, or conversational AI companion.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **Bookeyond Kid**. Tagline: **Learn Today, Build Tomorrow.** Both fixed.
- Visual world: **clearly Cambodian** — Khmer script, lotus and local nature, children who look like Cambodian families — treated with care, not as decoration.
- The logo, mark, colours, typography and illustration are open for redesign (owner delegated). Artwork: original illustration owned by the product (owner: "whatever is best").
- Reference: the owner's Figma (art direction only — layered paper-cut storybook collage, a path of books, warm dusty palette). Not a layout or component source.
- Learning Buddy is an authored character, never a chatbot.

## Evidence on Hand

- No testimonials, customers, reviews, partner schools, statistics or press exist. None may be invented.
- Real product screens and flows exist and are verified (see BOOKEYOND-KID-ENGINEERING-PROGRESS.md).

## Product Principles

1. Evidence before claims: show what answers show, and how strongly.
2. Warm for children, calm for adults: each role gets the experience its job needs.
3. Honest by default: nothing fake, nothing promised that isn't built.
4. Every child can use it: touch, keyboard, reduced motion, readable contrast.
5. Cambodian at heart, carefully made.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA (automated axe gate on every main screen, desktop and phone). Reduced motion respected. Touch targets sized for small hands. Khmer text uses a Khmer-optimized face.
