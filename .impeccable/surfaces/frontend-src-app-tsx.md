Audience/job: children 4–9 (Cambodian families first) learn in short sessions; parents read evidence and act; educators author and run schools. Constraints: PRODUCT.md; accessible names used by the E2E suite stay stable; no invented claims.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Blue hour on the Mekong — Cambodia just after sunset, cut from layered paper in deepening blues, where the only warm things are light: lanterns, fireflies, the glowing current book on a path of books. Light means "you, here, now, well done". Refuses the category default of bright rounded cards, a mint mascot and stat tiles on cream.

OWN-WORLD: Blue is the identity: a nine-step "Tonle" blue from night ink #0B1633 to mist #EEF3FB; paper white #FBFAF7 cooled, not cream; one warm light saffron #F3A83B (and ember #E0782F) used only for light, focus and success; lotus #E48BA0 and palm #2E7A5A as quiet subject/nature accents. Layered paper-cut SVG planes with a soft cast edge shadow and grain. Type: Bricolage Grotesque (display, optical sizes) + Kantumruy Pro (Khmer and Latin text, Cambodian-designed). Evidence strength drawn as line weight. Staff tools: slate-blue precision, hairline rules, dense tables, brand only in details.

STORY: A child steps into a dusk landscape, follows lit books to the next one, and makes it glow. A parent reads, in plain language with counted evidence, what their child can do and why the next step was chosen. An educator works in a calm, dense, professional tool.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Child home — full-bleed blue-hour landscape; the path of books winds from foreground to horizon; finished books sit lit, the current book glows saffron with the firefly buddy hovering above it; the greeting and one large "Play" lantern button sit bottom-left on the scene. Landing — the same world as a wide panorama with the brand, tagline and one primary action; the product's real evidence sentence shown beside it.

FORM: Pick card (paper-cut storybook, path of books, #1 on my list) fused with the rolled Sbek direction's light-through-carving feedback and its raises (depth planes, line-weight evidence, one question owns the stage, one shared cut grid). Seed key 2f3c820b.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
