Scope: the whole Bookeyond Kid web app (landing, auth, child, parent, teacher, principal, admin/Studio). Modes: landing = Persuade; child home = Experience; child activity = Operate at its simplest (one task owns the screen); parent/staff = Operate.

Audience/job: children 4–9 (Cambodian families first) learn in short sessions; parents read evidence and act; educators author and run schools. Constraints: PRODUCT.md; accessible names used by the E2E suite stay stable where behaviour is unchanged; no invented claims; real data only.

## Direction contract

THESIS: River Garden — a Cambodian village morning drawn as a storybook: a path of books winds through rice paddies, past a Khmer stilt house and sugar palms, to a lotus pond, and a family of animal Learning Buddies (Momo the otter leads) walks it with the child. Refuses the night-blue world it replaces and the category default of mascot-on-cream card grids and stat tiles.

OWN-WORLD: celadon-tinted rice-paper ground; Tonle deep river teal for structure and adult action; marigold sun for "now / next / done well" only; rice greens, clay-tile terracotta, lotus pink and palm-wood brown as the world's materials. Illustrated planes lit from the upper left with soft shading, no outlines, consistent proportions. Bricolage Grotesque display, Kantumruy Pro text (Khmer and Latin). Adult tools: light paper rail, hairline rules, dense tables, brand in details.

STORY: A child steps into the morning village, sees their buddy beside the book that is next, and presses one big button. In an activity the world falls away to a quiet field: one question, big answers, one next step. A parent reads what their child can do, how sure the evidence is, and what helps next. Staff work in a calm, dense, professional tool.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Child home — full-width storybook scene: paddies in the foreground, the path of books curving up to the stilt house, sugar palms framing the right, the chosen buddy standing by the current book; greeting and one large Play button bottom-left on a soft paper panel. Landing — the same village as a wide panorama with the brand, tagline, Momo and the buddy family, and one primary action.

FORM: brief-pinned world (owner's Figma "River Garden" study with Momo the otter, rebuilt with a new environment); no direction roll, the brief pins the world.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
